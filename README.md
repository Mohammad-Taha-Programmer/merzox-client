# Merzox

Merzox is an e-commerce application that is intended to group both businesses and their customers in the same place. In Merzox, you can explore businesses actions, prices, offers, products and services, and initiate purchase orders. Merzox facilitates communication by enabling users to:
1. Search for businesses and products.
2. track orders.
3. Chat live with businesses for further information and queries.
4. Locate nearby businesses and service providers on a dedicated live map
5. construct a profile that keeps your information private and secure to facilitate doing your business and purchase transactions.
